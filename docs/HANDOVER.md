# Business and Technical Handover

## What internal teams receive
- Data contract and governed feature definitions
- Reproducible training job and model artifact
- Model card, validation metrics and decision threshold
- Versioned scoring output for CDP and Power BI
- Monitoring thresholds, alerts, rollback and retraining procedure

## Stakeholder acceptance checklist
- Churn definition and 60-day horizon approved
- Retention capacity and risk thresholds approved
- CLV assumptions approved by Finance/CRM
- CDP field mapping and refresh SLA tested
- Privacy, security and fairness review completed
- Named operational owners have run the pipeline without the project author

## Suggested retention experiment
Randomise eligible high-risk members into treatment and control groups. Measure incremental retained members, incremental margin, contact cost and offer cost. Model quality alone does not prove business value; uplift must be demonstrated.
